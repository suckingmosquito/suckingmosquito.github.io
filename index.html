Cat vs. dog image classifier

Personal project — computer vision

Trained a CNN on the classic Kaggle cats-and-dogs dataset. The dataset itself turned out to be part of the lesson: a handful of corrupt JPEGs crashed training partway through, which meant writing a cleaning step before the model would even run. The first working version scored 99% on training data but only 83% on validation — a clear case of overfitting. Adding dropout, data augmentation, and early stopping brought validation accuracy up to 88% and closed the gap between the two.
